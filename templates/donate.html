Questions

Before you give

Is my gift tax-deductible?

No. Utah Civic Compact is a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization, which lets us lobby and take positions on legislation. Contributions are not tax-deductible as charitable donations.

Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, any time. Stripe emails a receipt for every charge. To change the amount or stop, email {{email}} and we'll take care of it.

Who sees my donation?

Our staff see the details they need to send a receipt. The public donor list on this page shows only a first name and an amount, and only if you leave that box ticked at checkout.

Is the checkout secure?

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe, not to us, and we never store it.
