The Stratos Project

We're fighting to stop it, and we need your support.

Utah Civic Compact is aiming to invalidate the Stratos Project's adoption on technical grounds rather than debating the merits of the project itself. This is often the most effective way to challenge administrative actions but we need your support to make this stick. Our arguments are sound but we do not have the financial backing that the project does. Updates on our fight will be posted here.
